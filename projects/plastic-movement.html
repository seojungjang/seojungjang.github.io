Study 01

Plastic Movement

What happens when the performer is removed and the environment itself becomes the primary presence?

Context

A performance created under pandemic conditions in which the audience entered the space without a live performer.

Question

What happens when the performer is removed and the environment itself becomes the primary presence?

Method

Light, spatial sequencing, audience timing, and environmental composition.

What this opened

The project shifted my attention from lighting a performer toward asking how an environment itself can carry presence, sequence attention, and shape interpretation.

Role

Lighting Designer

Credit

Performance project

Tools / Media

Light · spatial composition · audience choreography

Study 01

Building the environment

The suspended translucent structures, haze, and distributed light sources turned the room itself into the material of the performance. This installation view shows the spatial apparatus behind the audience experience.

Spatial elements

  1. Suspended translucent enclosures
  2. Haze as visible spatial material
  3. Distributed light sources and reflections
  4. A viewer path through changing environmental states
Study 02

Changing states

The same physical environment could read as materially and emotionally different through changes in color, direction, intensity, and haze. The comparison became an early way of thinking about environment as an active perceptual system.

WARM STATE / environment as presence
COOL STATE / same spatial apparatus
Reflection

What this changed

This project shifted my attention from lighting as illumination toward the environment as an active participant in performance.
