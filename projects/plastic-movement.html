Immersive Music Exhibition

Plastic Movement Chapter I

What happens when the performer is removed and the environment itself becomes the primary presence?

Context

Created under pandemic restrictions, the installation admitted one visitor at a time and had no live performer or operator. Six compositions formed a 30-minute loop through 16 individual speaker channels; each visitor chose their own path.

My approach

I programmed light, haze, and air movement into a timed sequence coordinated with sound. Without a performer to light, the room and its changing visibility became the material of the encounter.

Archive / timing

Reading the programmed score

The archived Eos printout records 105 cue starts before the 30:00 restart; 64 occur between 10:00 and 15:00. E5 appears early, E1 is repeatedly started and stopped in the dense middle passage, and E3 has no cue reference.

This is a record of programmed control, not visitor experience. No synchronized visitor path or sound record survives, so it cannot tell me which relationships among light, sound, and movement each person encountered.

01 / Space

Building the environment

Translucent structures caught the beams and haze gave them volume. Distributed speakers let a visitor's position change the mix heard within the same programmed sequence.

Spatial elements

  1. Suspended translucent structures
  2. Light and haze shaping visibility
  3. Individual speaker channels distributed through the room
  4. ETC Eos and QLab timed control
02 / States

Changing states

These photographs show warm and cool conditions within the same apparatus. Changes in color, direction, and haze altered the visible space; the images do not establish how a visitor interpreted it.

Reflection

What this changed

The score was fixed, but each visitor moved through it differently. I began to think about light, sound, haze, and movement as a composition of relationships, rather than separate effects.

Next question

Which relationships gave this performer-less environment its sense of presence, and which could change without losing that character?
