Study 06

Alone

How can lighting shift attention between the live body, AI-generated language, and the surrounding visual environment?

Project

Presented at the 26th Seoul International Dance Festival (SIDance) in 2023, Alone brought together live dance, lighting, projection, and AI-generated language. AI-generated words and phrases around difficult-to-name emotions were used as creative material for the performance, while the final choreographic, visual, and theatrical decisions remained human-authored.

My question

How can lighting shift attention between the live body, AI-generated language, and the surrounding visual environment?

My approach

Lighting cues, color temperature choices for the performer, the timing of transitions in relation to AI-generated text and projection, and decisions about when to isolate the body with light and when to combine it with projected media.

What this opened

A question of decision authority: what changes when AI-generated material no longer simply informs a human-authored performance, but is allowed to make bounded choices that alter what becomes visible?

Reflection

What this changed

This work shifted my interest from AI as generated material toward a question of decision authority: what changes when a computational system is allowed to affect the performance itself?

Next question

Alone used AI-generated language as creative material, while the final artistic decisions remained human. It led me to ask what might change if a computational system were given limited authority over the performance itself. What happens when a system is allowed to decide when a performer is revealed, withheld, or transformed into projection?
