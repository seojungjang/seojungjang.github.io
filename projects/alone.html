Contemporary dance / 2023

Alone

How can lighting shift visual emphasis among the live body, AI-generated text and imagery, and the surrounding environment?

Context

Presented at SIDance in 2023, Alone combined contemporary dance with AI-generated text and imagery. A custom GPT-4-based system generated textual continuations from a choreographer's prompt; human collaborators made the final artistic and cueing decisions.

My approach

I designed and operated lighting cues that alternately isolated the live body and placed it in a shared visual field with the projection. I chose color temperatures and timed transitions in relation to the projected material.

Surviving record

A lighting plot, performance photographs, a seven-minute edited highlight, and a live-operation photograph remain. The grandMA2 show file is unavailable, so exact cue values and timing cannot be reconstructed from these materials.

Reflection

What this changed

In Alone, the body, projected material, and light shared each moment. I became interested in how their timing changed the character of a movement.

Next question

If the movement stayed the same but the light arrived earlier or later, what would change in the moment, and what would remain?
